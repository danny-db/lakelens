Measure what you saved

A fix isn't real until the bill moves. These scoreboards read your billing tables so you can show a before and an after.

Read savings honestly, in three currencies. Efficiency (a run reads less / finishes faster) is always measurable. Capacity (freed headroom, shorter queues) only becomes a saving once you capture it by sizing down. Dollars are the scoreboard — shown here as Databricks list-price cost, so read them relative to your own contract (Finance applies your discount and, for classic compute, the separate cloud-infrastructure bill).
🏷️ Tag coverage

You can't attribute what you haven't tagged.

Tags are the foundation of cost attribution — without them, spend is one anonymous number you can't split by team, project, or environment. This finds what's untagged and lets you set a tag right here. Finding is read-only; setting a tag is the one action that changes a workspace setting, so it always asks first.
